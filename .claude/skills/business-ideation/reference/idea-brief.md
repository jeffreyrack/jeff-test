# Idea Brief Template

Ideation agents write every idea in this exact structure. Evaluators rely on the field names.

```markdown
## <ID> — <Idea name>

- **Persona:** <which ideator produced it>
- **One-liner:** <what it is, for whom, in one sentence>
- **Customer:** <specific buyer: who they are, where they hang out online, what they already pay for>
- **Problem / desire:** <the pain or want, in the customer's own words if possible>
- **Offering:** <exactly what is delivered: digital file, SaaS, report, physical item via POD, service…>
- **Price & revenue model:** <price point(s), one-time vs subscription vs per-use>
- **Physical items required?** <No / Yes, with how they are made and shipped and by whom>

### Upfront capital
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| … | $… |
| **Total** | **$…** |

### Monthly running costs (excluding operator tokens)
<itemized; hosting, APIs, fees, % payment processing>

### Customer acquisition plan
<the core, repeatable way customers find and buy this, step by step>

### Initial traffic approaches (at least 3, ranked by expected speed)
1. <channel> — <tactic> — <why it would work early with zero audience> — <expected speed>
2. …
3. …

### Path to first sale
<concrete sequence from day 0 to first $; ideator's own estimate of days to first sale>

### Who does what
- **AI operator:** <tasks>
- **Human sponsor:** <one-time tasks / recurring tasks with hours>

### Moat — why not just ask ChatGPT?
<honest answer>

### Biggest risks (ideator's own view)
<3 bullets>
```
